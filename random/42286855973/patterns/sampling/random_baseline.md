**What we see:** 3,500 random main-namespace non-redirect articles drawn in 8 rounds of list=random (exclusions: {'disambiguation': 195}); 3,500 collected, 2,943 existed by 2021-01-01, 557 were created later (flagged, kept out of the panel), 0 deleted since the draw. **Chart:** table: frame counts and lead length by group; data: data/random_baseline.json (summary)

**So what:** The baseline is a probability sample of English Wikipedia articles, so its drift is the site-wide drift a topic effect must exceed.

**Caveat:** list=random is not seedable; the stored frame is the sample. Random pages are mostly far from the topic, so critical terms are rare there: the baseline measures site-wide drift (style, bots, policy), not drift on comparable contested topics; that is what the comparison group is for.
