## Caveats

- The population is what the named WikiProjects assess on PageAssessments at the population run; articles no project tags are outside it. Task forces and optional projects that were not found are listed in data/mass3/population.json (`skipped_projects`).
- Reused mass2 rows were measured on mass2's date; fetched rows on this run's date, so their post-war windows differ by the gap between the two runs.
- Leads come from the mass_narr lead measure for both sources (mass2's `narr` snapshots), not the text re-measurement used by analyze_mass2, so unweighted figures here need not equal the mass2 cards.
- Full-text measures (b') are not collected in mass3.
- Pre-specify these estimands before treating them as confirmatory (AUDIT section 6).
