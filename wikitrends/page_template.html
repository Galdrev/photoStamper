Method and limits

How this was measured

  • Data. Every revision since 1 January 2021 from the Wikipedia API, plus a full-text snapshot of each article on the first day of every quarter, its talk-page activity and monthly pageviews. Collection ran on GitHub Actions.
  • Critical frames are terms tied to the anti-colonial reading: . Connection frames are terms tied to Jewish historical continuity and persecution: . Terrorism and militant are counted separately.
  • Text. References, templates, tables and markup are stripped before counting, so citation titles do not inflate counts. The lead is everything before the first section heading.
  • Test. Difference-in-differences: each article's change in rate, averaged by group, topic minus comparison. 2,000 bootstrap resamples give the interval.
  • Limits. Counting words measures emphasis, not accuracy. "Genocide" can grow because a court case exists, not because of bias. Sampled category articles vary in quality. Lexicons miss paraphrase and sentence-level stance. Editors are reported only in aggregate. No claim is made about any individual editor's intent.
