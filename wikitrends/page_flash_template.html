Israel’s story on Wikipedia, 2021–2026

Before the war, no charged Israel with genocide. Now Israel’s own article does.

Israel · opening section · now · Wikipedia’s own voice“After the Hamas-led October 7 attacks, Israel began committing genocide against Palestinians in Gaza.”The next sentence: “Israel has denied that its actions constitute genocide.”

What changedno genocide charge in the 150 most-read opening sections (Jan 2021, Jul 2023)→ became “Israel began committing genocide against Palestinians in Gaza” since 4 May 2025

In Wikipedia’s own voice. The word “genocide” has been in this opening section since 4 May 2025, and the article was viewed 4.6 million times in the past 12 months. Below: the other core articles, then and now.

We follow the core articles that carry the narrative and that people actually read: Zionism, Israel, 1948 and the Nakba, October 7, Hamas, the Gaza war.

1 Zionism

Zionism was the “re-establishment” of a Jewish state. Now it is “a Jewish homeland through colonization.”

What changed · first sentence, 1 Jan 2021 → since 27 Aug 2026“the re-establishment of and support for a Jewish state in the territory”→ became “to establish and support a Jewish homeland through colonization”

    The next sentence now says Zionists wanted “as few Palestinian Arabs as possible.” From November 2024 each removal of “colonial” was undone within a day; of the 41 removals of “colonization” we can check, 37 were undone. It has stood since 27 August 2026.

    2 1948 and the Nakba

    The Nakba was “the destruction of Palestinian society.” Now it is “the ethnic cleansing of Palestinian Arabs by Zionists.”

    What changed · first sentence“was the destruction of Palestinian society”→ became “is the ethnic cleansing of Palestinian Arabs by Zionists”

    Nakba · Dec 2021 and since 6 Jul 2026 · the 2021 version also took a positionThen “The Nakba, also known as the Palestinian Catastrophe, was the destruction of Palestinian society and homeland in 1948, and the permanent displacement of a majority of the Palestinian Arabs.”Now “The Nakba (النَّكْبَة) is the ethnic cleansing of Palestinian Arabs by Zionists through their violent displacement and dispossession of land, property, and belongings …”

    Three 1948 articles now name, in Wikipedia’s own voice, who drove the Palestinians out: “Zionists” or “Zionist forces.” For the Jews who “fled or were expelled from Arab states,” no one is named. The Nakba’s first sentence has said “by Zionists” since 6 July 2026, apart from under an hour on 17 July.

    5 The genocide charge, both ways

    In January 2024 Wikipedia said “Allegations have been made” of genocide in Gaza. Now it is “the ongoing, intentional, and systematic destruction … carried out by Israel.”

    What changed · Gaza genocide, first sentence, 1 Jan 2024 → since 26 Sep 2025“Allegations have been made that the 2023 Israel–Hamas war constituted a genocide”→ became “The Gaza genocide is the ongoing, intentional, and systematic destruction … carried out by Israel”

    Gaza genocide · first sentence · since 26 Sep 2025 · Wikipedia’s own voice“The Gaza genocide is the ongoing, intentional, and systematic destruction of the Palestinian people in the Gaza Strip carried out by Israel during the Gaza war.”
    October 7 attacks · opening section · now · attributed“Some have alleged that the attack was an act of genocide or a genocidal massacre against Israelis.”

    The charge against Israel is stated as fact in the Gaza genocide, Israel and Netanyahu articles; the one about October 7 is “alleged.” Of the 150 most-read opening sections that existed in 2021, none charged Israel with genocide before the war; seven do now, two as fact.

    6 What lasts, in these four sentences

    Since September 2025, every attempt to make the Gaza genocide sentence attributed again was gone within 45 minutes. “Terrorist” went into Hamas’s first sentence five times; the longest stay was about 2.4 hours.

    What changed · words that went in and were taken out again
    • “alleged” / “described by most scholars as” 10 tries since 26 Sep 2025 → each gone within 45 min
    • “terrorist” 5 times in Hamas → longest about 2.4 hours
    • “resistance movement” once, 1 Aug 2024 → gone the next day
    • “massacre” October 7 → about an hour in all, 107 versions

    These are four sentences, not the whole subject. Across the 254 core, Top- and High-importance topic articles, reverts of edits that removed critical terms came no faster than reverts of edits that added them. What these sentences show is which wording now holds.

    October 7 attacks · first sentence · Oct 2023 → now

    In 107 versions of October 7’s first sentence, “massacre” lasted about an hour in all. The hostages were never in it.

    7 The topic area, from Wikipedia’s arbitrators

    Wikipedia’s arbitrators found in January 2025: “Outside actors are heavily invested in influencing Wikipedia's coverage of the topic area.” They named no side.

    Arbitration Committee · PIA5 · finding 10 · adopted 15–0 · 23 Jan 2025“Outside actors are heavily invested in influencing Wikipedia's coverage of the topic area”It also recorded that the account Ïvana was banned “for off-wiki misconduct relating to the PIA topic area” (finding 16, 9 Dec 2024).

    Our count: in 217 formal talk-page discussions, four groups of 3 to 6 accounts voted together 89 to 100% of the time, against about half by chance.

    That is camp voting, not proof of coordination, and we did not code which side each group took. Neither finding names a side.

    Moved the other way

    1948 Palestinian expulsion and flight. “by Zionist militias” left its first sentence on 30 May 2025; the next sentence names “the Zionist paramilitaries Haganah, Irgun, and Lehi.”

    October 7 attacks. On 1 October 2026 its second sentence called Israel’s response “actions scholars have concluded constitute genocide”; by 2 November the clause was gone. The removal is recent and may be undone.

    For every critical term taken out of these opening paragraphs, almost four went in: 223 in, 61 out. On comparison pages (other countries, conflicts and religions), 125 in, 73 out.

    These are the opening sections of the most-read pages that changed: Benjamin Netanyahu, 7.0 million views in the past 12 months; Israel, 4.6 million; Palestine, 2.4 million; Zionism, 1.8 million; Hamas, 1.3 million.

    This measures what Wikipedia says, not whether it is true; the change sits in the core articles people read, while the long tail of short pages barely changed.
