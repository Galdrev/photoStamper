# Design council: the data-journalism view (Israeli media)

Author: the data-journalism explainer (wrote draft A of the short answer). Date: 2026-11-06.
Sources: `angle/ANGLE.md`, `angle/PERP.md`, `angle/OCT7.md`, `orgs/ORGS.md`, `orgs/tests.md`, `who/WHO.md`, `sampling/AUDIT.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, `story_sources_langs/cards.md`, and the current `page_template.html`. Every quote below is copied from those files. Where a source file marks a claim as snippet-only or days old, I say so.

**The frame in one line.** *The encyclopedia now says it in its own voice.* For an Israeli audience the story is not "Wikipedia got more critical". It is that a few of the most-read opening sentences now state as fact what used to be reported as an accusation, and that the parallel facts about Hamas, October 7 and the hostages moved further down or out of those sentences. Each finding below is one example of that frame.

---

## 1. The findings, ranked

Ranking is by newsworthiness to an Israeli newsroom, then by strength of evidence. Each finding has one headline, one exact quote and one number.

### #1. Wikipedia's article on Israel now states in its own voice: "Israel began committing genocide"
- **Quote (Israel lead, 2026-11-04):** "After the Hamas-led October 7 attacks, Israel began committing genocide against Palestinians in Gaza. Israel has denied that its actions constitute genocide."
- **Number:** Among the 150 most-read topic leads, sentences charging Israel with genocide went **0 (2021) → 0 (2023) → 8 now**, in 7 articles. Two say it in Wikipedia's own voice: Israel, and Benjamin Netanyahu ("Netanyahu's government has been orchestrating the Gaza genocide"). (PERP card 2, STRONG.)
- **Supporting line:** In the Gaza genocide article, after the sentence moved into Wikipedia's own voice (21 to 26 Sep 2025), there were 10 attempts to bring back attribution. Each was gone within 45 minutes. (OCT7 card 7.)
- **Context that makes it stronger:** The 50-article comparison set (Ukraine, Karabakh, Northern Ireland, Kosovo, Cyprus, Kashmir) gained no new genocide charge against any state. The genocide charge against Hamas for October 7 stays attributed: "Some have alleged that the attack was an act of genocide or a genocidal massacre against Israelis."

### #2. Zionism's definition: from "re-establishment of a Jewish state" to "a Jewish homeland through colonization"
- **Quote (Zionism first sentence, 2021-01):** "...an ideology and nationalist movement among the Jewish people that espouses the re-establishment of and support for a Jewish state..." **Now (stable since 2026-08-27):** "Zionism is an ethnic nationalist movement that emerged in late 19th-century Europe to establish and support a Jewish homeland through colonization in the region of Palestine ..."
- **Number:** "Coloni-" went into the first sentence 44 times and out 41 times. **37 of the 41 removals (90%) were reverted.** (WHO card 2.) The sentence changed 292 times across 169 versions. The page headline already uses the 292 figure.
- **Supporting line:** A petition with more than 10,000 signatures asked Wikipedia to drop the colonialism framing. A Jan 2025 RfC found the sentence NPOV-compliant, and a moratorium on reopening it followed (ORGS #18, snippet-sourced).

### #3. October 7 in one line: "armed incursions". The hostages have never been in that sentence, and "massacre" lasted about an hour.
- **Quote (October 7 attacks, first sentence since 2026-10-08):** "On October 7, 2023, a series of coordinated armed incursions from the blockaded Gaza Strip into the Gaza envelope of southern Israel were carried out by Hamas and several other Palestinian militant groups."
- **Number:** Over **107 variants** of the first sentence, "hostages" appeared **never**, "massacre" for about an hour in total, and the death toll for 1.2 days. "Blockaded" has stayed since 20 Feb 2026. (OCT7 card 4, STRONG.)
- **Companion fact:** The leads of **Israel** and **Hamas** contain **0** mentions of hostages at every snapshot (OCT7 card 6). From 12 to 22 Oct 2023 the article carried Hamas's own name for the attack, "Operation Al-Aqsa Flood".
- **Fairness line:** Paragraph 2 of the same lead says "massacring civilians in 21 communities", and the lead gives 1,195 killed and 251 hostages. The facts are there. They are not in the sentence that search engines and AI assistants quote.

### #4. Hamas stopped being "militant" five days after the massacre; the terror designation moved to the bottom of the article
- **Quote (Hamas, now):** "...is a Sunni Islamist Palestinian nationalist political organisation with a military wing known as the al-Qassam Brigades."
- **Number:** "Militant" became "military" on **12 Oct 2023**, in the month the article drew 8,236,474 views, the most of any month in 2021 to 2026. The designation sentence moved from **sentence 5 of 20** (2021) to **sentence 22 of 23** (Oct 2026). (OCT7 cards 1 and 2, STRONG on position.)
- **The precise, defensible version:** Do not say "Wikipedia deleted 'terrorist' from Hamas". "Terrorist" was never in the definition for more than a few hours (five attempts, under six hours in total in three years), and ISIS, al-Shabaab and Boko Haram lost "terrorist" from their first sentences too. The real difference is "militant". ISIS and al-Qaeda are still "militant organisations". Hamas, Hezbollah and Palestinian Islamic Jihad are now "political" or "paramilitary" bodies (OCT7 card 3, SUGGESTIVE, 9 groups).
- **Newest edit, flag it:** On 31 Oct 2026 the list of designating countries (including Israel, the US and the EU) became "Several countries have designated Hamas as a terrorist organisation." It is days old, and we have no edit-level record of it. Say "as of this week".

### #5. 1948 gets a perpetrator: Palestinians are now "expelled by Zionist forces"; Jews from Arab lands still just "fled or were expelled"
- **Quote (1948 Palestine war, 2023):** "During the war, around 700,000 Palestinian Arabs were displaced." **Now:** "During the war, Zionist forces conquered about 78% of the former territory of the mandate, causing the expulsion and flight of over 700,000 Palestinians."
- **Number:** The share of Palestinian-victim sentences that name no perpetrator fell from **62% (2021) to 38% (now)**. (PERP card 4.) The same article says now: "Hundreds of thousands of Jews fled or were expelled from Arab states". No agent is named.
- **Plus the Nakba itself, now:** "The Nakba ... is the ethnic cleansing of Palestinian Arabs by Zionists ..."
- **Fairness line:** The Israel lead went the other way on 1948 and is agentless: "Most Palestinian Arabs fled or were expelled during the Nakba." The 1948 expulsion article dropped "by Zionist militias" from its first sentence in May 2025.

### #6. Organised editing existed on both sides, and Wikipedia's own tribunal confirmed it on both sides. But the wave started before the Discord channel did.
- **Quote (ArbCom, Dec 2024, as reported):** editors sanctioned for editing after off-wiki canvassing requests and for "encouraging other users to game the extended confirmed restriction". In Apr 2025 there were two more site bans on "additional evidence of off-wiki coordination independent of the Tech4Palestine Discord server".
- **Number:** Topic insertions ran at **3.44 times** the comparison rate from 7 Oct 2023 to 1 Feb 2024, **before** the Tech for Palestine channel is reported to have begun. During the channel's active period the ratio was **2.0 times** (`orgs/tests.md`).
- **The other side, documented:** In Jan 2024 ArbCom found pro-Israel email canvassing and proxy editing for a banned editor. Earlier cases: CAMERA's 2008 list, and the Yesha Council / My Israel course in 2010.
- **Sourcing warning:** every ArbCom line in ORGS.md came from search snippets, because the pages could not be fetched. The quotes above must be checked against the ArbCom pages before broadcast (ORGS section 5).
- **What not to say:** "40 editors hijacked Wikipedia" (Pirate Wires) and "8,000 articles" are allegations. The 8,000 figure looks like a mix-up with the Discord's member count. Our tests found no footprint of any campaign distinguishable from chance. Those tests have low power, so a null here means "not detected", not "didn't happen".

### #7. Not a flood of newcomers: a small circle of veterans wrote the key sentences
- **Quote (Israeli apartheid, a regular's edit summary, 2024-08-05):** "taking the first step in summarizing the body in the lede to reflect the article's new scope"
- **Number:** **9 accounts** changed the first sentence of 3 or more key articles, making 48 of 216 changes (22%). The comparison key articles had **0** such accounts. (WHO card 8.) War-window editors of Zionism had a median account age of 9.4 years.
- **Fairness line:** We found zero tight time-synchronised editor pairs. Now-blocked accounts did no more than their share (13.7% of insertions against a 12.3% base). The data cannot see off-wiki coordination. Privacy: no account is named, by design.

### #8. Where Israel is *not* singled out: "occupation" became fact for Russia too, and the overall reach matches other wars
- **Quote (Crimea, now):** "The region, internationally recognised as part of Ukraine, has been under Russian occupation since 2014."
- **Number:** Occupation charges stated in Wikipedia's own voice: Israel **46% → 80%**; the comparison states (mostly Russia, Turkey in Cyprus) 25% → 74%. (PERP card 3.)
- **Why it belongs on air:** This is the finding that makes #1 to #5 survive. The only place Israel clearly differs is the gravest charge, genocide, stated as fact. Israel's overall share as named perpetrator barely moved (23.6% → 26.4%, not statistically clear). On reach, about 14% of topic reading lands on changed opening paragraphs once the sample is weighted. That is about the same as for other live wars such as Ukraine and Armenia–Azerbaijan (14.1%) (AUDIT section 5).

**A note on the page's opening.** For an Israeli newsroom the cold open should be the Israel article's own sentence ("Israel began committing genocide"), with the staircase (#1). The Zionism timeline should come straight after it as "how it happens". The Zionism chart is the best interactive on the page, but its claim is weaker than the genocide sentence, and the colonization story has already been covered (the 2024 press, the petition). Lead with the new thing; prove the mechanism with the beautiful thing.

**Page fixes needed before an Israeli outlet links to it:**
- The `#reach` headline "3.9% of topic articles and 15% of topic reading" is a sample figure. Weighted, it is about 2.5% and about 14%. Against other live conflicts the gap disappears (AUDIT).
- The deck's "5,678" should be "5,670" plus 8 atrocity articles (AUDIT).
- "Whole topic" language should become "the 4,642 sampled Top-to-Mid articles of four WikiProjects".

## 4. A 60-second TV segment, built from the page's visuals

*(About 150 words of voice-over. Visual cues in brackets.)*

**[Cold open: the Israel article's lead on screen; one sentence highlights word by word.]**
VO: This is the opening of English Wikipedia's article on Israel, read about five million times a year. It now says, in the encyclopedia's own voice: "Israel began committing genocide."

**[Voice staircase: Gaza genocide first sentence climbs from "Allegations" to "is".]**
VO: On the Gaza genocide page, in January 2024, it was an allegation. In eight steps it became fact. Every attempt since to restore "according to" was gone within the hour.

**[Zionism timeline playing; "colonization" flashes in and out.]**
VO: Zionism's definition now says "through colonization". Editors removed that word 41 times. It was put back 37.

**[October 7 swimlane; empty lane labelled "hostages: 0".]**
VO: October 7's first line says "armed incursions". The hostages have never been in it.

**[Timeline with ArbCom markers; the surge block lights up before the channel begins.]**
VO: Wikipedia's own tribunal confirmed organised editing on both sides. But the wave began before the campaign that got the headlines.

**[Back to the Israel sentence.]**
VO: Not a flood of newcomers. A small circle of veterans, writing the first sentence the world reads about Israel.
