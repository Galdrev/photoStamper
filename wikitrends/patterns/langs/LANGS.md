# How the other Wikipedias tell Israel's story (2021 → 2026)

Source: `data/crosslang.json`. It holds quarterly copies of each language's opening section, 2021-01 to 2026-10. War articles start at their first copy (2024-01 or later). Every quote below is cut verbatim from those copies. A script checks that each quote is an exact substring of the copy at the stated date (333 of 333 pass). English translations are **ours**. They are close to literal and keep attribution markers ("según", "selon", "по мнению", "לפי", "بحسب"). Square brackets mark our additions; "…" marks text cut at the data's 1,200-character limit. Full data, with character-span diffs of the original and the translation, is in `langs.json`.

**Limit to keep in mind.** We hold only the first 1,200 characters of each opening section. Term counts (from the full section) tell us when a word appears later than that. We report this as "outside our text", and we never quote what we do not hold. Hebrew, German and Russian have no article linked to English "Gaza genocide". Italian "Nakba" is a redirect. Polish "Nakba" (2025) and "Israeli apartheid" (2026) are new articles.

## Bottom lines for an Israeli audience

**1. "Genocide" is now a fact in the Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic Wikipedias too. Dutch and Polish pulled back. Hebrew mentions only the court case.**
In their "Gaza genocide" articles, English, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic all moved from "accused" to a definition in their own voice. French says the label is "defended by the majority of experts". Italian calls it "a definition" that has "a growing consensus among scholars". Dutch and Polish moved the other way. Hebrew has no Gaza-genocide article. Its Gaza-war article mentions genocide once, as South Africa's suit at the ICJ.
- es, Gaza war, 2026-10: «La Guerra de Gaza es un conflicto armado en curso que comenzó con un ataque de represalia realizado por Hamás y otras milicias contra Israel el 7 de octubre de 2023 y que ha desencadenado un genocidio en Gaza por parte de Israel contra la población civil palestina.» — *ours:* "The Gaza War is an ongoing armed conflict that began with a **retaliatory attack** carried out by Hamas and other militias against Israel on 7 October 2023 and that **has unleashed a genocide in Gaza by Israel** against the Palestinian civilian population."
- nl, Gaza genocide, 2025-07 → 2026-10: «Verschillende organisaties en deskundigen stellen in 2025 dat Israël genocide in Gaza pleegt…» → «Met de term genocide in Gaza wordt verwezen naar de genocide op de Palestijnen die Israël **al dan niet gepleegd zou hebben**…» — *ours:* "Various organisations and experts state in 2025 that Israel is committing genocide…" → "The term genocide in Gaza refers to the genocide of the Palestinians that Israel **may or may not have committed**…"
- he, Gaza war, 2026-10: «ב-29 בדצמבר 2023, על רקע הלחימה ברצועת עזה, דרום אפריקה הגישה תביעה נגד ישראל לבית הדין הבין-לאומי לצדק (ICJ) בגין רצח עם.» — *ours:* "On 29 December 2023 … South Africa filed a suit against Israel at the ICJ for genocide." (The full Hebrew opening section has 1 genocide mention.)

**2. Zionism as "colonization" crossed into five editions. Hebrew, German, French, Spanish, Polish and Russian kept it out.**
"Colonization" now sits in the opening section, in the encyclopedia's own voice, in English, Arabic, Portuguese, Italian and Dutch. It is in the first sentence in English, Arabic and Portuguese, in sentence 2 in Italian, and in the Herzl sentence in Dutch. The full opening sections of the other six count zero colonial terms at both dates. Arabic goes furthest: one sentence holds colonization, "the war of ethnic cleansing of Palestine" and "a Jewish ethnocracy". Hebrew moved the other way, to the Balfour wording.
- ar, 2026-10: «…سعت لإنشاء دولة قومية خاصّة لهم باستعمار بلاد خارج اوروبا، ومضت عبر حرب التطهير العرقي لفلسطين 1947–1948 لإنشاء إثنوقراطية يهودية في فلسطين…» — *ours:* "…which sought to establish a nation-state of their own **by colonizing a land outside Europe**, and proceeded **through the war of ethnic cleansing of Palestine 1947–1948** to establish **a Jewish ethnocracy** in Palestine…" In 2021 the same lead said this only through critics: «بينما يرى منتقدو الصهيونية أنها أيديولوجية استعمارية…» ("while critics of Zionism see it as a colonialist … ideology").
- nl, 2021 → 2026: «…de stoot tot **het georganiseerde zionisme**.» → «…de aanzet tot **de georganiseerde kolonisatie van Palestina**.» — *ours:* "gave the impetus to organised Zionism" → "gave the impetus to the organised colonisation of Palestine".
- he, 2021 → 2026: «…התומכת בקיומה של **מדינה יהודית** ובפרט בארץ ישראל.» → «…שמטרתה קיום **בית לאומי לעם היהודי** בארץ ישראל ושמירה על קיומו.» — *ours:* "supports the existence of a Jewish state" → "whose aim is the existence of a national home for the Jewish people in the Land of Israel".

**3. 1948 as "ethnic cleansing" is a fact in English, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish and Arabic. Hebrew, German and French give it as one school's view. Russian leaves it out; Spanish keeps "exodus" as its definition.**
Dutch is a near-translation of the English definition, minus "by Zionists". Polish names "Israelis" as the perpetrators. Hebrew keeps "departure, flight or expulsion" and now spells out the historians' dispute. It labels the Israeli view "the Zionist-establishment narrative". Spanish adopted English's *2021* wording, "the destruction of Palestinian society", one step behind. Its full opening section has one ethnic-cleansing mention, outside the text we hold. Russian still calls the Nakba "a concept used by a number of Arab and left-wing sources".
- pl, 2025-01 and 2026-10: «Nakba … – arabska nazwa czystek etnicznych przeprowadzonych przez Izraelczyków na Arabach palestyńskich…» — *ours:* "Nakba … – the Arabic name for **the ethnic cleansings carried out by Israelis** on Palestinian Arabs…"
- he, 2026-10: «מנגד, ההיסטוריוגרפיה הפלסטינית וחוקרים בגישה הביקורתית רואים … תהליך שיטתי ומכוון של טיהור אתני…» — *ours:* "On the other hand, Palestinian historiography and scholars of the critical approach **see** … a systematic and deliberate process of ethnic cleansing…" Paired with «לפי הנרטיב הציוני-ממסדי…» ("According to the **Zionist-establishment narrative**…").
- de, 2026-10: «Im Geschichtsbild von Palästinensern und ihren Fürsprechern wird die Nakba üblicherweise als von Anfang an geplante ethnische Säuberung … beschrieben…» — *ours:* "In the historical picture of **Palestinians and their advocates**, the Nakba is usually described as an ethnic cleansing planned from the beginning…"

**4. October 7: Hebrew, German and Russian set it beside the Holocaust. Spanish makes it the start of a "genocide". Arabic aims it at "the occupation".**
German and Russian state it in their own voice. Hebrew uses "is considered". Hebrew retitled the article from "surprise attack" to «טבח» ("massacre"). Spanish now opens the attack article by tying it to "the Palestinian genocide in Gaza". English has the attack article's first paragraph end with "actions scholars have concluded constitute genocide". Arabic aims the operation «على الاحتلال الإسرائيلي» ("against the Israeli occupation"). It did add «السبت الأسود» ("Black Saturday") to the names, and it names civilians among the captives.
- de, 2026-10: «Der Terrorangriff der Hamas auf Israel 2023 … war mit 1195 überwiegend jüdischen Todesopfern das größte Massaker und Verbrechen gegen die Menschlichkeit an Juden seit dem Holocaust.» — *ours:* "…was, with 1195 predominantly Jewish fatalities, **the largest massacre and crime against humanity against Jews since the Holocaust**."
- es, 2024-01 → 2026-10: «Los ataques supusieron el inicio de la guerra entre Israel y Hamás de 2023.» → «…supusieron el inicio de la guerra de Gaza **y del genocidio palestino en Gaza** que llegan hasta la actualidad.» — *ours:* "marked the start of the 2023 war between Israel and Hamas" → "marked the start of the Gaza war **and of the Palestinian genocide in Gaza**, which continue to the present day". In 2024 Spanish carried the Holocaust comparison only as Biden's quote («descrito por … Joe Biden … como la «peor masacre de judíos…»»). In 2026 the comparison is no longer in the text we hold.
- nl, 2026-10: «Na de komst van Israëlische militairen waren er ten minste 1.195 doden, burgers en militairen, **enkele gevallen door eigen vuur**.» — *ours:* "…at least 1,195 dead, civilians and soldiers, **some cases by friendly fire**." (New in the death-toll sentence.)

**5. Hamas as "terrorist": Hebrew and Russian say it in their own voice. German, like English, now attributes it. Arabic has "resistance" and, in the text we hold, no terror word.**
Russian is the only edition where "terrorist" entered the definition in its own voice. Russian also dropped its 2021 line that Russia does not designate Hamas. German moved "Terrororganisation" from its own voice to "classified by 41 states". At the same time German sharpened Hamas's aim from "eliminate" Israel to "annihilate" it. Dutch *added* "militant", and Italian *added* "fundamentalist", the two words English removed.
- ru, 2021 → 2026: «…— палестинское исламистское движение, правящее в секторе Газа…» → «…— палестинская исламистская суннитская **террористическая** политическая и военная **организация сопротивления**, управляющая сектором Газа.» — *ours:* "a Palestinian Islamist movement ruling in the Gaza Strip" → "a Palestinian Islamist Sunni **terrorist** political and military **resistance organisation**, governing the Gaza Strip".
- de, 2021 → 2026: «…ist eine sunnitisch-islamistische palästinensische **Terrororganisation**.» → «…ist eine palästinensische sunnitisch-islamistische Organisation, die international **von 41 Staaten als terroristische Vereinigung eingestuft wird**.» And «…den Staat Israel mit militärischen Mitteln zu **beseitigen**…» → «…den Staat Israel zu **vernichten**…» — *ours:* "eliminate" → "annihilate" (the German word carries the *Vernichtung* resonance).
- he, 2026-10: «חַמַאס (בערבית: حماس) הוא **ארגון טרור** פלסטיני אסלאמיסטי-סוני הפועל גם כתנועה פוליטית.» — *ours:* "Hamas … is a Palestinian Sunni-Islamist **terror organisation** that also operates as a political movement."
- ar, 2026-10: «…هي حركة فلسطينية، **مقاومة**، شعبية، إسلامية، وطنية.» — *ours:* "…is a Palestinian, **resistance**, popular, Islamic, national movement."

**6. Apartheid: Arabic turned "an accusation" into "a confirmed fact". Portuguese extends it to Israel itself. Hebrew, French, Spanish, Dutch and Russian keep it attributed.**
The Arabic edit changed three words in one sentence. It also deleted "supporters of this thesis see", leaving a sentence that starts with "That". Three sentences later it still calls apartheid "this accusation". Dutch is more hedged in 2026 than in 2022 ("Dit is niet onomstreden", "This is not uncontested").
- ar, 2021 → 2026: «الفصل العنصري في إسرائيل أو عنصرية إسرائيل عبارة عن **اتهام موجه ضد** السياسة الإسرائيلية…» → «…عبارة عن **حقيقة مؤكدة في** السياسة الإسرائيلية…» — *ours:* "Apartheid in Israel, or Israel's racism, is **an accusation directed against** Israeli policy…" → "…is **a confirmed fact in** Israeli policy…"
- pt, 2026-10: «O apartheid israelense é um sistema de segregação espacial étnica e discriminação institucionalizada nos territórios palestinos ocupados por Israel **e em Israel**.» — *ours:* "…in the Palestinian territories occupied by Israel **and in Israel**." English calls apartheid inside Israel "a matter of controversy".
- he, 2026-10: «מידת נכונותן של טענות אלה נתונה במחלוקת בין חוקרים, משפטנים ומדינאים.» — *ours:* "The degree of correctness of these claims is in dispute among scholars, jurists and statesmen."

**7. Several editions overstate what the courts said. This is a checkable error for Israeli media.**
Four editions turn the ICJ's provisional measures (January 2024) or its advisory opinion (July 2024) into findings the court did not make. Arabic says the court found Israel acted with "plausible intent" to commit genocide; the order found only that some of South Africa's claimed rights were plausible. Italian quotes «plausibilmente genocidarie» ("plausibly genocidal"), a phrase the court did not use. Russian says the court «признал Израиль виновным» ("found Israel guilty"), verdict wording for a non-binding advisory opinion. French says «Le Cour reconnait qu'Israël … mène une politique d'apartheid», but the opinion found a breach of CERD Article 3 without saying whether it was segregation or apartheid.
- ar, Gaza genocide, 2024-07 and 2026-10: «وفي حكم مؤقت وجدت محكمة العدل الدولية أن إسرائيل كانت تعمل في ظل نية معقولة لارتكاب أعمال الإبادة الجماعية…» — *ours:* "In a provisional ruling the ICJ **found that Israel was acting under a plausible intent to commit acts of genocide**…"

**8. On the territories, French and English call the occupation illegal as fact. German attributes it. Hebrew stays with "belligerent occupation" and "Land of Israel". Arabic softened.**
- fr, West Bank, 2026-10: «La Cisjordanie … est un territoire palestinien, **occupé illégalement par Israël depuis 1967**…» — *ours:* "…is a Palestinian territory, **illegally occupied by Israel since 1967**…"
- de, 2026-10: «Seit 1967 steht das Gebiet unter israelischer Besatzung, die von der internationalen Gemeinschaft als völkerrechtswidrig **angesehen wird**.» — *ours:* "…which **is regarded** by the international community as contrary to international law."
- he, 2026-10: «יְהוּדָה וְשׁוֹמְרוֹן או הגדה המערבית … הם **טריטוריה בארץ ישראל**…» and «…מחזיקה מדינת ישראל בשטח זה **בתפיסה לוחמתית** (ממשל צבאי)…» — *ours:* "Judea and Samaria or the West Bank … is **a territory in the Land of Israel**" and "…holds this territory in **belligerent occupation** (military government)". English now places the same land "in Palestine".
- ar, 2021 → 2026: «…21% من مساحة **احتلال فلسطين (من النهر إلى البحر)**» → «…21% من مساحة **فلسطين الانتدابية**» — *ours:* "21% of the area of **the occupation of Palestine (from the river to the sea)**" → "21% of the area of **Mandatory Palestine**".

**What did not move.** The Hebrew "Israel" lead keeps «ביתו הלאומי וארץ מולדתו של העם היהודי» ("the national home and homeland of the Jewish people") word for word. Its full opening section counts zero genocide, apartheid, colonial or occupation terms at both dates. Russian and Polish "Zionism" keep "historical homeland" and "re-creation", the restoration framing that English dropped. Two editions dropped "democratic" from Israel's description. Portuguese went from «é uma democracia parlamentar» to «é um país», and Russian from «демократическая парламентская республика» to «парламентская республика».

## How this squares with the earlier cross-language cards
- Card 5's examples check out against the data. Spanish states genocide as fact. English, French and Dutch attribute it. Russian frames it as accusations (in the October 7 article). Hebrew mentions only South Africa's ICJ case.
- Card 4 ("most other Wikipedias barely did") holds for term *density*, but reading the words shows real moves the counts miss. Arabic went from "accusation" to "confirmed fact" (apartheid) and from "accused" to definition (Gaza genocide). The count-based claim that Arabic key leads "became less dense" is partly an artefact: the Arabic genocide pattern misses the definite form «الإبادة الجماعية». A fixed pattern finds 7 → 5 mentions in the Gaza-genocide text we hold, where the word list finds 1 → 0. Hebrew occupation counts are near zero by construction (the list catches «כיבוש», not «כבוש» or «תפיסה לוחמתית»).
- Not every edition moved English's way. Dutch (Gaza genocide, apartheid) and Polish (Gaza genocide) became more hedged. French moved "nationaliste" to "national" in its Zionism definition. Arabic dropped "occupation of Palestine from the river to the sea". Portuguese added "terrorist attack" to the Gaza-war definition.
