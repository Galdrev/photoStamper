# Angle brief: audience and lens (owner direction, 2026-11-06)

**Audience.** Israeli media: news-channel editors, journalists and their viewers.

**Lens.** How the world's main reference work, English Wikipedia, now tells Israel's story. What changed, how, when, who changed it, and how organised it was. What Israelis should understand about how the world sees them, through the text millions of people (and AI assistants) read first.

**Not neutral-for-neutrality's-sake.** Lead with what matters to an Israeli audience. Examples:
- the Zionism definition now says "through colonization", and the Jewish "re-establishment" link was removed;
- Wikipedia's own voice now says Israel "began committing genocide";
- 1948 is retold as ethnic cleansing "by Zionists";
- Hamas's definition went from "militant" to "a political organisation with a military wing", and the terror-designation sentence moved from sentence 5 to the end of the lead ("terrorist" itself never held in the definition for more than a few hours);
- documented off-wiki canvassing (the Discord channel, confirmed by Wikipedia's own arbitration body).
Name these plainly, in Israeli newsroom language (e.g. "the encyclopedia now states as fact...").

**Accuracy rules (unchanged and non-negotiable). This is what makes the piece survive critics:**
- Facts, numbers and quotes must be exact. No lies, no exaggeration, no claim stronger than the data.
- Say "documented" only for what is documented, "alleged" for allegations, and "not detected" where our tests found nothing. Explain why "not detected" is not "didn't happen" when power is low.
- It's fine to report that pro-Israel organised editing also existed. It makes the piece credible. It doesn't have to lead.
- Privacy: no unmasking of individual pseudonymous editors. Organisations and documented processes are fair game.

**Tone.** Confident, clear, Israeli-newsroom readable. Serious, not tabloid. Short sentences.

## Owner direction (2026-11-08): the subject is the narrative, not Wikipedia
Wikipedia is the vessel: the reference the world, search engines and AI assistants read first. Headlines, bottom lines and conclusions are about the TOPIC and the NARRATIVE: what the world's default story now says about Zionism, Israel, 1948, October 7, Hamas, the hostages, Palestinians and the charges against Israel. Statistics and Wikipedia mechanics (articles, edits, editors, importance tiers) come second, as the evidence. Example: "Zionism, as the world now reads it, is 'a Jewish homeland through colonization'", not "Wikipedia articles gained critical terms".
