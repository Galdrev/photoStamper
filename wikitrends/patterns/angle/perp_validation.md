# Perpetrator coding: validation

Coder: the same coder (Claude) who made the codes, re-reading each sampled sentence against the codebook. This is a self-check, not an independent second coder: it catches slips and rule drift, not the coder's shared biases. An independent check by a human (ideally an Israeli and a non-Israeli reader) is still worth doing on the 100 rows listed below.

## Codebook (short)

- **Kept sentence:** describes violence, harm, displacement, legal charges or rights. A keyword prefilter (2,786 candidate sentences) was used for recall; the coder kept 1,731 unique sentences (2,012 sentence-date rows). Pure mentions ("during World War II, he..."), religious-myth narratives (with a few exceptions coded as attributed) and definitions were dropped.
- **Perpetrator:** the agent of the harm *named in the sentence*, or picked up through a pronoun from the sentence before. Passive voice with no agent ("fled or were expelled") is coded **unclear/none**. This is the rule the regex attempt broke: labels go on the *agent*, never on the victim. Codes: I Israel/IDF/government (incl. state settlement policy), J Jews/Zionists/Yishuv and non-state Israeli Jewish actors (settler violence, Kach, Goldstein, Amir, Ben-Gvir's pre-ministerial acts), H Hamas/PIJ/Fatah/PLO/other Palestinian armed groups, P Palestinians/Arabs (civilian or general, Palestinian Authority), O other state or actor (Arab state armies, Nazi Germany, Rome, etc.), N unclear/none. A sentence can have several clauses (for example Hamas kills Israelis | Israel kills Palestinians). Mutual fighting ("a civil war between Arabs and Jews") is N>N.
- **Voice:** W = Wikipedia's own voice; A = attributed to a source or qualified ("according to", "considered", "described as", "likely", a court ruling, a designation by states, a poll); D = denied or disputed in the same sentence ("Israel has denied...", "not considered a terrorist organization by...", a claim followed by a counter-claim). When the harm itself is stated as fact and only a condemnation is attributed, voice is W for the harm; when the charge (e.g. "violating human rights", "genocide") is the attributed part, voice is A. Voice is recorded per charge; the headline charge is listed first.
- **Charge class:** genocide, ethnic cleansing/expulsion (incl. deportation and flight/expulsion), apartheid, occupation, terrorism, massacre/killing (incl. lethal attacks on people, bombings, rockets at towns), war crimes, antisemitism, other (wars, invasions, blockades, annexation, discrimination, colonization; colonization is flagged separately as `colonization_label`).
- **Comparison disputes:** side A / side B per dispute: Ukraine A=Russia/USSR/pro-Russian forces, B=Ukraine; Karabakh/Armenia A=Azerbaijan/Ottoman/Turkey, B=Armenia/Armenians; Cyprus A=Turkey/Turkish Cypriots, B=Greece/Greek Cypriots; Kosovo A=Serbia/Yugoslavia, B=Kosovo Albanians/KLA; Northern Ireland A=British state/unionists/loyalists, B=republicans/nationalists; Kashmir A=India, B=Pakistan/Pakistan-backed militants. Third parties (Crimean Tatars, NATO, Mongols) are O.

Sample A draws sentence-date rows, so two sentences that are unchanged across dates appear twice (Final Solution, Bloody Sunday). Codes shown are the codes at the time of the check; the three flagged rows are corrected in `perp_codes.jsonl`.

Both fields are above the 85% bar, so no full recode was needed. The three errors were fixed, and the same rule was applied wherever else it occurred: the two "invasion internationally condemned" sentences (W), the three "the war ended with the territory divided... Jordan annexed" sentences (an added O>P clause), and the two Entebbe "counter-terrorist mission" sentences (terrorism clause set to unclear). The other three Entebbe hijacking sentences that name the hijackers (PFLP-EO plus German Revolutionary Cells) are coded H+O.

**Recall check.** After coding, a second keyword sweep (hijack, rape, atrocity, assault, repression, abuse, evict, forced, punish, settler, Zionist, colonial, supremacist and others) over the same leads found 92 more candidate sentences. 10 of them were relevant and were coded and added (e.g. the Entebbe hijacking by PFLP-EO members, "Zionists wanted to create a Jewish state ... with as few Palestinian Arabs as possible", the forced removal of Karabakh Armenians). So the first prefilter missed roughly 0.6% of relevant sentences found overall (10 of 1,731). Recall for sentences with no keyword at all was not measured.

**Known judgement calls** (a critic could code these differently): settlers and Kahanist activists as J rather than I; Fatah/PLO fighters as H; "Palestinian rocket attacks" as H; "referred to as Israeli apartheid" (Israeli–Palestinian conflict, now) as own voice, because the sentence asserts the system of discrimination as fact and names no source; "described as an annexation" as attributed; poll results ("60% of Palestinians support armed attacks") as attributed.
